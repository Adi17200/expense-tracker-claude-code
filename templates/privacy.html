Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, such as your email address. We also collect the financial transactions you manually enter into the app, including amounts, categories, and dates.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the expense tracking service. This includes generating your spending summaries, categorizing your expenses, and maintaining your user profile.

3. Data Storage

We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your information is stored securely on our servers and is only accessible to you through your authenticated account.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers that compromise your privacy.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your data, please contact us at privacy@spendly.example.com.
